# Elevation

<ScaleTable
  prefix="--elevation"
  sample={ElevationSample}

Elevation roles are exported as `shadow-glass`, `shadow-raised`,
`shadow-overlay`, and `shadow-hairline`.

Dark surfaces also use surface lightness to communicate elevation.

  <div className="rounded-card bg-surface p-4 shadow-raised">

  <div className="rounded-card bg-surface-raised p-4">

  </div>
</BothThemes>

## Glass

`glass` contains the shared translucent background, edge, shadow, and blur.
`Card variant="glass"` and `Button variant="secondary"` reuse it.
